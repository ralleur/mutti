Ralf Hauser maintains the Mutti product identity. Open-source copyright

release, endorsement or affiliation. This policy claims no registered trademark

## Permitted references

You may truthfully discuss Mutti, link to the project, say your software is
based on or compatible with it, and show screenshots with the applicable
credits. Personal builds and unchanged official redistribution may be
identified accurately. Do not imply that Ralf provides support for your
distribution.

For a modified or independently branded public product, use a distinct name,
logo and application icon. A factual "based on Mutti" credit is welcome. Do not
use the Mutti or ralleur identity in a way that suggests your release, domain,
store listing or service is official without permission.

## Scope and provenance

The identity includes the Mutti name, wordmark, symbol and app icon documented
in [mutti/design](mutti/design/README.md), including their copies in the web
client, the Mac app bundle and the onboarding pages. The ralleur identity
belongs to the broader Ralleur project identity; its transfer must be
expressly agreed and is not implied by a transfer of Mutti.
